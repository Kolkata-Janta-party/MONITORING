PRIVACY

Your data stays in your browser.

This static dashboard does not include analytics, advertising, account tracking, or a server-side monitoring database. Endpoint configuration and health results are stored locally with browser localStorage.

When you add a live endpoint, your browser sends the request directly to that endpoint. The endpoint operator may receive the normal network information associated with that request. Do not add private URLs, credentials, API keys, tokens, cookies, or other secrets.

Demo endpoints are simulated locally and do not contact example.com. GitHub Pages hosts the static files; it does not receive your dashboard's localStorage contents through this application.
